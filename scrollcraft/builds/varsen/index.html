II

Built in the order a mechanic would

  1. Frame

    Carbon, aero-profiled. The down tube deepens from 49 to 62 mm into the bottom bracket.

  2. Fork

    Slides up into the head tube from below: 44.4 mm of offset, 53.5 mm of trail.

  3. Wheels

    Rolled in along the ground, the front from ahead, the rear from behind with its cassette on. 45 mm rims, 26 mm tyres.

  4. Drivetrain

    52/36 rings and 170 mm cranks that pedal forward as they arrive. The chain is solved link by link: 112 links, every roller seated.

  5. Cockpit

    A one-piece bar and stem, 100 mm at −6°, lowered onto the steerer as it turns into line.

  6. Saddle

    725 mm from the bottom bracket, along the seat tube. The bike is whole.

III

In detail

Close enough to count the chain links. Every part is modelled, down to the bolts.

Drivetrain

52/36 chainrings on 170 mm cranks, into an 11–30 twelve-speed cassette. The chain is solved link by link: 112 links, every roller seated.

Rear end

The brake side: a 140 mm rotor and its caliper, and a 142 mm thru-axle through slotted dropouts.

Cockpit

One piece of carbon: a 100 mm stem at −6° and a 420 mm bar, taped through the drops.

The whole

That is the size 56. Next, it rebuilds itself at yours.
